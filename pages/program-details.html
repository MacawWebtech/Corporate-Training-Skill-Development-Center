FAQ

Common questions

Is prior leadership training required?

No. We meet people where they are and differentiate by experience in the room.

Can we customize cases?

Yes for private cohorts — we adapt examples to your industry and challenges.

What’s the ideal group size?

12–20 for rich discussion; smaller or larger groups can be accommodated.

Do participants get a certificate?

Yes — every participant receives a completion certificate, and your training coordinator receives a cohort summary.
